About the Programme

NDA Preparation Built for Dombivli Students

The NDA Entrance Exam, officially the NDA & NA Examination, is conducted twice a year by the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) to select candidates for training at the National Defence Academy and the Indian Naval Academy. It gives students the opportunity to join the Army, Navy or Air Force soon after Class 12, and to grow into leadership roles through structured academic and military training.

Our Dombivli centre supports students through the full journey the written examination covering Mathematics and the General Ability Test, followed by the SSB interview and medical examination. Success calls for strong fundamentals in Mathematics, sharp reading and reasoning skills for the GAT, consistent practice with previous-year questions, and honest self-assessment through mock tests.

Preparing after Class 12 comes with its own pressures board exams, limited time, and an unfamiliar exam pattern. Our structured coaching approach helps students build concepts methodically, track weak areas, and practise under exam-like conditions, while beginning SSB awareness early rather than as an afterthought.

Which Wing Are You Targeting

NDA Wings & Career Paths

Candidates appearing for the NDA Entrance Exam apply for one of three services, each leading to a distinct career path. Exact eligibility conditions for each wing should always be confirmed from the latest official UPSC notification, as these can be revised between examination cycles.

Ground Forces
Army

Trains cadets for future roles as commissioned officers in the Indian Army, with emphasis on leadership, ground operations and command responsibilities. Generally does not require Physics and Mathematics at Class 12.

Maritime
Navy

Trains cadets for future roles as officers in the Indian Navy. Because naval training involves technical and engineering concepts, Physics and Mathematics at Class 12 are typically required.

Aviation
Air Force

Trains cadets toward future roles as officers in the Indian Air Force, similarly requiring a Physics and Mathematics background. Not every candidate who joins becomes a pilot; roles vary by later selection stages.

Joint Training
National Defence Academy

Cadets from all three services train together at the tri-service National Defence Academy before moving to their respective service academies for branch-specific training.

Exact eligibility, vacancies and requirements for any NDA wing can vary between notifications. Always verify current details from the official UPSC notification.

Who Can Apply

NDA Eligibility Criteria

NDA Entrance Exam eligibility is determined by UPSC and published afresh with every notification, covering nationality, age, educational qualification, marital status and physical and medical standards.

Educational Qualification

Candidates who have passed or are appearing in Class 12 are generally eligible. Army wing candidates are generally not required to have studied Physics and Mathematics, while Navy and Air Force wing candidates generally are, based on current norms.

Age Limit

Recent UPSC notifications have specified an age window of approximately 16.5 to 19.5 years as on the course-commencement date. The exact date-of-birth range changes for every notification (NDA I and NDA II are notified separately) always check the current cycle.

Nationality & Marital Status

Candidates are generally required to be Indian citizens, or meet specific conditions applicable to Nepal, Bhutan or other categories defined by UPSC, and are generally required to be unmarried at the time of application and through training.

Physical & Medical Standards

Candidates must meet height, weight, vision and general fitness standards prescribed by the Armed Forces medical authorities, confirmed at the medical examination stage after the SSB.

Eligibility conditions can be revised between examination cycles. Always cross-check the latest official UPSC notification before applying.

Syllabus

What the NDA Written Exam Covers

The NDA Entrance Exam written examination consists of two papers — Mathematics and the General Ability Test (GAT). The exact distribution of topics is specified by UPSC in the official notification for each cycle.

Mathematics Syllabus

Algebra (sets, relations, quadratic equations), Matrices and Determinants, Trigonometry, Analytical Geometry (two and three dimensions), Differential Calculus, Integral Calculus and Differential Equations, Vector Algebra, Statistics and Probability, Logarithms, Complex and Binary Numbers, Sequence and Series, and Permutations and Combinations.

General Ability Test (GAT) Syllabus

Part A — English: grammar, vocabulary, comprehension, correct usage and sentence structure. Part B — General Knowledge: Physics, Chemistry, General Science, History, Geography and Current Events, tested at a level appropriate to a Class 10–12 student.

  • English preparation — daily reading of newspapers and editorials, regular grammar revision, timed comprehension practice, and a personal error notebook.
  • General Knowledge preparation — History, Geography, General Science and Current Affairs as a continuous, cumulative habit rather than a one-time revision exercise.
  • Objective format — both papers are objective-type and subject to negative marking.
  • Language policy — Mathematics is typically set in English, while the GAT paper is typically set bilingually in English and Hindi, subject to the current notification.

Where NDA Leads

Careers After NDA

NDA is a pathway toward officer-level training in the Armed Forces, not an automatic guarantee of a commission. Selected candidates train jointly at the National Defence Academy before moving to service-specific training at their respective academy.

Indian Army

Roles centred on leadership, command and the management of personnel and operations, with career progression through successive ranks.

Indian Navy

Roles combining specialised technical training with leadership responsibilities aboard naval assets.

Indian Air Force

Roles combining leadership responsibilities with possible aviation, technical branch or other specialisations depending on training outcomes.

National Defence Academy Training

Academic education, physical conditioning and military training designed to build discipline, leadership and teamwork before branch-specific training begins.

GICE Academy does not guarantee NDA selection, SSB recommendation, rank, score, commission or a defence job. Preparation outcomes depend on each candidate's individual performance across all official stages.
