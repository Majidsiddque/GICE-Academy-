Exam Structure

Understand the Banking Exam Stages

1
Preliminary Examination

Practise core subjects under timed conditions and focus on accuracy, speed and question selection.

2
Main Examination

Prepare for the subjects and advanced topics required by your chosen examination.

3
Interview or Final Stage

Where required, prepare for the interview or other applicable stages in the recruitment process.

Exam stages and subjects vary by recruitment. Always check the latest official notification for your target examination.

Syllabus

Important Banking Exam Subjects

Common preparation areas — exact pattern varies by exam
Subject Key Topics
Quantitative Aptitude Arithmetic, percentages, ratio, averages, number series and data interpretation
Reasoning Ability Puzzles, seating arrangements, syllogisms, inequalities and logical reasoning
English Language Grammar, vocabulary, reading comprehension and sentence structure
General and Banking Awareness Banking terminology, financial awareness, current affairs and static general knowledge
Computer Awareness Basic computer and information technology topics, where included in the exam

Question counts, section timings and syllabus differ between IBPS, SBI and RBI examinations. Refer to the current official notification when planning your revision.

Frequently Asked Questions

Banking Coaching in Dombivli FAQs

Which banking exams can I prepare for?

Depending on your target and the available course, preparation may cover IBPS PO, IBPS Clerk, IBPS RRB, SBI PO, SBI Clerk and RBI examinations. Contact GICE Academy to confirm current course coverage.

How long does banking exam preparation take?

Preparation time depends on your existing knowledge, study schedule and target examination. Ask the academy about current batch duration and study plans.

What subjects should I study for banking exams?

Common subjects include Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning Ability and English Language. Depending on the examination, you may also need General Awareness, Banking Awareness and Computer Awareness.

Are mock tests important for banking exams?

Mock tests help you practise under timed conditions, assess your performance and identify subjects that need more revision.

Can working professionals prepare for banking exams?

Working professionals can prepare with a consistent study plan. Contact GICE Academy to ask about available class timings and batch options.

How can I enquire about banking coaching fees?

Contact GICE Academy by phone or email, or use the enquiry form to request information about fees, batches and admission.
