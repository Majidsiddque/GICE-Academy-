SSC Coaching

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) conducts some of the most sought-after government recruitment exams in India, opening the door to roles in ministries, departments, and public sector offices. At GICE Academy, our SSC coaching covers Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, English, and General Awareness with structured lessons, regular practice sets, and full-length mock tests designed around the latest exam pattern.

Exam Pattern Overview

General Tier-Wise Pattern (Varies by Exam)
Tier Subjects Covered Mode
Tier I General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude, English Comprehension Computer-Based Test
Tier II Advanced Quant, English Language, General Studies, Statistics / Finance (post-specific) Computer-Based Test
Skill / Document Verification Typing test, skill test, or document verification depending on the post applied for In-Person

Exact tiers, subjects, and cut-offs vary by exam (CGL, CHSL, GD, MTS) — our counsellors will walk you through the specific pattern for the exam you're targeting.
