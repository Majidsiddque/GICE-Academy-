About the Programme

MBA CET Preparation Built for Thane Aspirants

MBA CET is the common name for the MAH MBA/MMS CET — the Maharashtra Common Entrance Test for admission to MBA and MMS programmes, conducted by the State Common Entrance Test Cell, Maharashtra (State CET Cell), under the Government of Maharashtra.

MBA CET is a computer-based test assessing Logical Reasoning, Abstract Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, and Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension. With four sections, 200 questions and just 150 minutes on the clock, it rewards students who prepare with a clear plan rather than scattered effort.

Our Thane centre supports MBA CET aspirants through concept building, structured practice, realistic mock tests, personalised faculty guidance, and continuous mentorship, following a simple philosophy: Practise → Test → Analyse → Correct → Revise → Retest → Improve.

Exam Structure

The Four MBA CET Sections

MBA CET tests four distinct skill areas within a single, un-sectioned 150-minute window. Candidates may attempt sections in any order.

75 Questions
Logical Reasoning

The highest-weightage section — arrangements, puzzles, syllogisms, coding-decoding and analytical reasoning.

25 Questions
Abstract Reasoning

Figure series, pattern recognition, visual analogies and non-verbal, entirely pattern-based reasoning.

50 Questions
Quantitative Aptitude

Mathematics and data interpretation — arithmetic, algebra, geometry, and data from tables and charts.

50 Questions
Verbal Ability & RC

English, vocabulary, grammar and reading comprehension.

Exact question counts, section-wise weightage and marking scheme can be revised by the State CET Cell each year. Always verify current details on cetcell.mahacet.org before finalising your preparation plan.

Who Can Apply

MBA CET Eligibility Criteria

It's important to distinguish between eligibility to appear for MBA CET and eligibility for admission to a specific MBA/MMS programme through CAP — the two are not the same.

Educational Qualification

A bachelor's degree of at least three years' duration, in any discipline, from a recognised university, with a minimum aggregate of 50% (45% for backward class/PwD candidates from Maharashtra).

Final-Year Students

Candidates in their final year of graduation may also apply provisionally, subject to completing their degree before the admission process concludes.

Age & Work Experience

There is generally no upper age limit prescribed. Work experience is not mandatory, though some institutes may give limited additional weightage during CAP.

Maharashtra State vs All India Candidature

MS candidature generally requires state domicile-related requirements and offers reservation benefits; AI/Outside-Maharashtra candidates can also appear and participate in CAP under the All India category.

Eligibility for MBA CET is different from eligibility for a specific programme. Always verify both from the current official notification on cetcell.mahacet.org.

Syllabus

What the MBA CET Sections Cover

The topics below represent the major preparation areas commonly tested across MBA CET's four sections. The exact mix can vary — treat these as areas to prepare for, not a guaranteed question-wise breakdown.

Logical Reasoning

Arrangements and seating arrangements, puzzles, blood relations, direction sense, coding-decoding, number and letter series, syllogisms, statement and conclusion, statement and assumption, and data-based reasoning.

Abstract Reasoning

Figure series, pattern recognition, figural analogies, visual reasoning, figure classification, odd-figure-out questions, embedded figures, missing figures and series completion — entirely visual and pattern-based.

  • Quantitative Aptitude — number system, arithmetic, time and work, time-speed-distance, interest, algebra, geometry, mensuration, probability, permutation-combination and data interpretation.
  • Verbal Ability & RC — reading comprehension, vocabulary, grammar, sentence correction and completion, synonyms/antonyms, para-based questions and sentence arrangement.
  • Logical Reasoning weightage — carries the most questions (75) of any section, making it a high-priority area.
  • No official past papers — exact papers are not officially released after the exam; previous-year-style practice helps gauge difficulty and question style.

Common Questions

MBA CET Coaching in Thane — FAQ

What is MBA CET?

MBA CET is the common name for the MAH MBA/MMS CET — the Maharashtra Common Entrance Test conducted by the State CET Cell, Maharashtra, for admission to MBA/MMS programmes across the state.

What is the MBA CET exam pattern?

MBA CET consists of 200 MCQs across four sections, to be completed in 150 minutes, for a total of 200 marks, with +1 for each correct answer and no negative marking.

What is the MBA CET eligibility criteria?

A bachelor's degree in any discipline (minimum three years) with at least 50% aggregate marks (45% for backward class/PwD candidates from Maharashtra). Final-year students may also apply provisionally.

What is the MBA CET CAP process?

CAP (Centralized Admission Process) is the official counselling mechanism that follows the exam — covering registration, document verification, merit list publication, choice filling, seat allotment and admission confirmation.

Which colleges accept MBA CET scores?

A large number of MBA/MMS institutes across Maharashtra participate in MBA CET-based admissions through CAP, including several well-known, long-established B-schools. Always verify the current list on cetcell.mahacet.org.

Does GICE Academy in Thane help with MBA CET mock tests and sectional practice?

Yes — sectional tests, topic tests and full-length mock tests simulating the real 150-minute, un-sectioned format, along with detailed performance analysis, are built into the preparation journey.
