About the Programme

NMAT Preparation Built for Dombivli Aspirants

NMAT by GMAC (NMIMS Management Aptitude Test) is a national-level, computer-based management entrance exam conducted by the Graduate Management Admission Council (GMAC) the same body that administers the GMAT. Its scores are used by NMIMS Mumbai and a wide network of other business schools in India and abroad for admission to MBA and PGDM programmes.

NMAT is attractive because of its flexible testing window, adaptive format, absence of negative marking, and the option to attempt the exam more than once in a single cycle. But flexibility is not the same as ease a strong NMAT score still depends on solid fundamentals, accurate timing, and consistent, well-analysed practice.

Our Dombivli centre works with NMAT aspirants through every stage of this journey concept building, guided practice, sectional and full-length mock tests, performance analysis, and personalised faculty mentorship, following a simple philosophy: Learn → Practise → Test → Analyse → Improve → Perform.

Our Approach

What Sets Our NMAT Coaching Apart

NMAT rewards candidates who can move quickly and accurately across three distinct, individually timed sections. Our Dombivli programme is built around the things that genuinely convert practice into a stronger score.

Exam Structure

The Three NMAT Sections

NMAT tests three broad skill areas within a strict 120-minute, individually timed structure. Candidates may be permitted to choose the order in which they attempt the sections, subject to the rules for the current cycle.

28 Minutes
Language Skills

36 questions covering reading comprehension, vocabulary, grammar, sentence correction and error identification.

52 Minutes
Quantitative Skills

36 questions covering arithmetic, algebra, geometry, number system and data interpretation.

40 Minutes
Logical Reasoning

36 questions covering analytical and verbal reasoning, arrangements, coding-decoding and critical reasoning.

Adaptive
Computer-Based Format

NMAT is a computer-based, adaptive test where question difficulty responds to how a candidate is performing, scored on GMAC's scaled model.

Exact question counts, section timings, scoring scale and negative-marking policy can be revised by GMAC between cycles. Always verify current details on mba.com/exams/nmat before finalising your preparation plan.

Who Can Apply

NMAT Eligibility Criteria

NMAT eligibility is set by GMAC and is intentionally kept broad. Individual business schools that accept NMAT scores often layer on their own, more specific eligibility requirements.

Educational Qualification

A bachelor's degree (typically 10+2+3 or 10+2+4 format) in any discipline from a recognised university, with a minimum aggregate generally around 50%, subject to relaxation for reserved categories at many institutes.

Final-Year Students

Students in their final year of graduation, or awaiting results, are generally eligible to apply on a provisional basis.

Age & Work Experience

There is currently no upper age limit for NMAT candidates. Work experience is generally not mandatory, though some institutes may give it additional weightage.

Exam Eligibility vs College Eligibility

Meeting NMAT's basic eligibility does not guarantee you meet a specific business school's admission requirements always check each target institute's own eligibility page.

Eligibility conditions can be revised between cycles. Always cross-check the latest official NMAT by GMAC notification before applying.

Syllabus

What the NMAT Sections Cover

GMAC does not publish a single fixed syllabus document for NMAT. The topics below reflect the major preparation areas that have consistently appeared across previous testing cycles.

Language Skills

Reading comprehension, vocabulary and word usage, grammar and sentence correction, error identification, para-jumbles and para-completion, sentence formation, and identifying main idea, inference and tone.

Quantitative Skills

Arithmetic (percentages, profit and loss, interest, ratios), time-speed-distance and time-work, number system, algebra, geometry, mensuration, averages, probability, permutation and combination, data interpretation and data sufficiency.

  • Logical Reasoning — analytical and critical reasoning, arrangements and puzzles, series, coding-decoding, blood relations, direction sense, syllogisms and data sufficiency.
  • Adaptive question bank — since NMAT draws from a large question bank, not every topic appears in every candidate's paper.
  • No negative marking — under the current marking scheme, every correct answer is awarded marks with no penalty for wrong or unattempted questions.
  • Section-order choice — where confirmed for the current cycle, candidates may choose the order in which they attempt sections.

What is the NMAT exam?

NMAT (NMAT by GMAC) is a national-level, computer-based management entrance exam conducted by GMAC for admission to MBA/PGDM programmes at NMIMS and a wide network of other participating business schools.

What is the NMAT exam pattern?

NMAT has three sections — Language Skills, Quantitative Skills and Logical Reasoning — totalling 108 questions in 120 minutes, with no negative marking under the current scheme.

What is the NMAT eligibility criteria?

A bachelor's degree in any discipline with a minimum aggregate generally around 50%, from a recognised university. Final-year students can generally apply provisionally, and there is currently no upper age limit.

Can NMAT be taken more than once?

Under current NMAT rules, candidates are generally permitted to attempt the exam more than once within the same testing cycle. Always verify exact attempt limits on the official NMAT by GMAC website.

Which colleges accept NMAT scores?

NMAT scores are accepted by NMIMS and a wide network of other participating business schools, commonly cited at 50 or more institutes. Always confirm current acceptance directly with each institute.
