XAT, IBSAT & CMAT Coaching

GICE Academy provides structured preparation for MBA entrance examinations, combining concept-based learning, regular practice, mock tests, strategy sessions and guidance for aspirants.

XAT

XAT, conducted by XLRI, Jamshedpur, is a national-level management entrance examination for MBA and PGDM programmes. GICE Academy's preparation focuses on Decision Making, Verbal & Logical Ability, Quantitative Ability & Data Interpretation and General Knowledge.

IBSAT

IBSAT is the ICFAI Business School Aptitude Test. It assesses areas including data adequacy, logical reasoning, reading comprehension and quantitative techniques. GICE Academy focuses on concept clarity, practical problem solving and comprehensive practice.

CMAT

CMAT, conducted by the National Testing Agency, is a national-level entrance examination for MBA and PGDM admissions. Preparation covers Quantitative Techniques & Data Interpretation, Logical Reasoning, Language Comprehension, General Awareness and the applicable Innovation & Entrepreneurship component.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I prepare for XAT, IBSAT and CMAT together?

Yes. The exams share important aptitude foundations, particularly in quantitative, verbal/language and reasoning skills. Exam-specific preparation should then be added for the individual format and requirements.

What does GICE Academy's MBA entrance preparation cover?

The preparation approach includes concept building, regular practice, sectional tests, mock tests, performance analysis, strategy sessions, study support and mentorship.

Which areas should I prepare first?

Begin with quantitative aptitude, verbal or language skills, logical reasoning and data interpretation fundamentals, then move into timed and exam-specific practice.

Does GICE Academy offer preparation for other MBA entrance exams?

Yes. GICE Academy's website lists CAT, NMAT, SNAP, MBA CET, XAT, CMAT, GMAT and IBSAT among its MBA entrance courses.
