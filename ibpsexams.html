Build Your Career Through IBPS Exams

The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection conducts recruitment examinations for several participating public sector banks in India.

IBPS examinations provide opportunities for candidates interested in banking careers through different recruitment streams such as Probationary Officer, Clerical Cadre and Specialist Officer.

A systematic preparation strategy can help candidates strengthen their fundamentals, improve speed and accuracy, and become familiar with computer-based examination patterns.

Why Prepare Systematically?

IBPS examinations test multiple abilities within limited time. Preparation should therefore combine concept learning, regular practice, mock tests, revision and performance analysis.

IBPS Examinations

Explore the major IBPS recruitment examinations and understand their preparation requirements.

PO

IBPS PO

Probationary Officer / Management Trainee recruitment is designed for candidates interested in officer-level banking positions.

  • Reasoning Ability
  • Quantitative Aptitude
  • English Language
  • General & Banking Awareness
  • Descriptive Writing
SO

IBPS SO

Specialist Officer recruitment provides opportunities in specialized banking roles.

  • IT Officer
  • Agriculture Officer
  • Law Officer
  • HR / Personnel Officer
  • Marketing Officer
CL

IBPS Clerk

Clerical recruitment is suitable for candidates interested in clerical and customer-service oriented banking roles.

  • English Language
  • Numerical Ability
  • Reasoning Ability
  • General / Financial Awareness
  • Computer Aptitude

IBPS PO Examination

Understand the major preparation areas and recruitment stages for IBPS PO.

Major Preparation Areas

  • Reasoning: Puzzles, seating arrangements, syllogism, inequalities, coding-decoding and logical reasoning.
  • Quantitative Aptitude: Simplification, number series, arithmetic, data interpretation and quadratic equations.
  • English: Grammar, vocabulary, reading comprehension, cloze tests and error detection.
  • Awareness: Current affairs, banking terminology, financial awareness and general knowledge.

Selection Stages

Candidates generally progress through multiple stages of the recruitment process.

  • Preliminary Examination
  • Main Examination
  • Interview / final selection stage

Preparation Focus

Candidates should work on both accuracy and speed while gradually increasing the difficulty of practice questions.

IBPS Selection Process

Divide your preparation according to the different stages of the examination.

1

Preliminary Exam

Strengthen basic concepts and practice solving questions accurately within the available time.

2

Main Exam

Develop advanced problem-solving ability, banking awareness and strong sectional preparation.

3

Interview

Prepare banking-related topics, current affairs and communication skills where applicable.

Regular sectional tests and domain-specific practice can help identify topics that need additional attention.

IBPS Preparation Strategy

Follow a consistent preparation routine combining learning, practice, revision and performance analysis.

01. Build Concepts

Start with the fundamentals and understand basic methods before attempting advanced questions.

02. Practice Daily

Solve questions regularly and maintain a consistent study schedule.

03. Attempt Mocks

Take sectional and full-length tests to become comfortable with timed examinations.

04. Analyse Results

Review mistakes, identify weak topics and modify your preparation accordingly.

Important Areas to Prepare

Give regular attention to these important areas during your IBPS preparation.

R

Reasoning

Focus on puzzles, seating arrangements, inequalities, syllogism, coding-decoding and logical reasoning.

Q

Quantitative Aptitude

Practice arithmetic, simplification, approximation, number series, quadratic equations and data interpretation.

E

English

Improve grammar, vocabulary, reading comprehension, cloze tests and sentence correction.

G

General Awareness

Revise current affairs, banking awareness, financial developments and important national and international events.

C

Computer Awareness

Study computer fundamentals, operating systems, internet basics, networking and common applications.

M

Mock Tests

Use timed mock tests to evaluate preparation, improve question selection and develop examination discipline.
