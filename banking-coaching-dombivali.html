Exams We Cover

Banking & Financial Exam Categories

Banking recruitment in India runs through several distinct examinations, each with its own conducting body and post structure.

IBPS
IBPS PO

Conducted by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection every year for 20 PSU banks, for the recruitment of Probationary Officers.

IBPS
IBPS Clerk

A common exam held by IBPS for 20 PSU banks, for the recruitment of Clerical Cadres.

IBPS
IBPS SO

Recruits Specialist Officers — Marketing Officer, IT Officer, HR Officer and similar roles — across 20 PSU banks.

IBPS
IBPS RRB

A common written exam for all Regional Rural Banks of India, for Probationary Officer, Clerical and Specialist Officer posts.

SBI
SBI PO

State Bank of India conducts its own exam for recruitment of Probationary Officers.

SBI
SBI Clerk

SBI's own annual exam for recruitment into Clerical Cadres.

RBI
RBI Grade B

RBI recruits officers at Grade B through an all-India exam — a limited but genuinely prestigious opportunity.

RBI
RBI Assistant

RBI recruits Clerical Cadre staff through its own all-India exam.

Exam Structure

Banking Exam Stages

The journey through a banking entrance exam is structured into three critical stages: Preliminary exams, Mains, and a final Interview — each testing something different.

1
Preliminary Exams

Fundamental concepts in Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning and English language proficiency.

2
Mains

A deeper dive into advanced Quant, Reasoning, Data Analysis and Banking Awareness, including SBI PO/IBPS PO-specific knowledge.

3
Interview

Communication skills and banking-sector knowledge, supported by dedicated interview guidance and mock panels.

Syllabus

Bank Exam Syllabus

The banking exam syllabus is designed to evaluate intelligence and problem-solving ability through aptitude-based questions, across both the Preliminary and Mains stages.

Mains Exam Syllabus

  • Quantitative Aptitude — advanced-level questions challenging numerical skills.
  • Reasoning Ability — higher-order reasoning with complex puzzles and problem-solving.
  • English Language — advanced grammar and comprehension proficiency.
  • General Awareness — current affairs, banking awareness, and static GK relevant to the sector.
  • Basic IT Knowledge — understanding of basic computer operations and IT relevant to banking.
