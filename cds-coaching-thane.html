About the Programme

CDS Preparation Built for Thane Graduates

The CDS Entrance Exam, conducted twice a year by UPSC, is one of India's leading routes for graduates who want to become commissioned officers in the Armed Forces — opening the door to four training academies: the Indian Military Academy, the Indian Naval Academy, the Air Force Academy and the Officers' Training Academy. Our Thane centre exists to help graduates in the area prepare for both halves of that journey: the written examination, and the SSB interview that follows it.

Every candidate who joins begins with a diagnostic assessment across English, General Knowledge and, where the target academy requires it, Elementary Mathematics. That baseline shapes a personalised study plan built around one philosophy: Learn → Practise → Test → Analyse → Improve → Revise.

Because qualifying the written paper only makes a candidate eligible for the SSB stage — not a guarantee of selection — preparation at GICE Academy also builds early awareness of officer-like qualities, communication and teamwork, alongside regular subject-wise practice, previous-year questions and full-length timed mock tests.

Where CDS Leads

The Four CDS Training Academies

The CDS Entrance Exam leads to four distinct training academies, each tied to a specific service, eligibility profile and written-paper structure. Candidates should confirm exact, current requirements — including gender eligibility, which has been periodically revised — from the latest official UPSC notification.

Indian Army
Indian Military Academy (IMA)

Permanent commission in the Army. Generally open to graduates in any discipline. Written exam: English, GK & Elementary Mathematics.

Indian Navy
Indian Naval Academy (INA)

Permanent commission in the Navy. Generally requires an Engineering degree, given the technical nature of naval roles.

Indian Air Force
Air Force Academy (AFA)

Permanent commission in the Air Force. Generally requires Physics & Maths at Class 12, or an Engineering degree.

Indian Army — SSC
Officers' Training Academy (OTA)

Short Service Commission. Open to graduates from any discipline — no Elementary Mathematics paper in the written exam.

Gender eligibility, along with all other academy-specific criteria, should always be verified against the latest official UPSC CDS notification before applying.

Who Can Apply

CDS Entrance Exam Eligibility

CDS eligibility differs across IMA, INA, AFA and OTA rather than following one single, universal standard — candidates should identify their target academy first, then verify the exact conditions for that academy.

Educational Qualification

A degree in any discipline for IMA and OTA; a degree in Engineering for INA; a degree with Physics & Mathematics at Class 12, or an Engineering degree, for AFA. Final-year students can generally apply provisionally.

Age Limit

Fixed separately for every cycle through a precise date-of-birth window. Recent cycles have used approximate ranges around 19–24 years for IMA/INA, a similar band for AFA, and a slightly wider range for OTA.

Nationality, Gender & Marital Status

Generally Indian citizens (with specific provisions for Nepal, Bhutan and certain other categories). OTA is generally open to both men and women; IMA, INA and AFA have generally been open to male candidates, subject to the current notification. IMA/INA/AFA candidates are generally required to be unmarried.

Eligibility conditions genuinely differ by academy and are published afresh with every UPSC notification. Always verify current details from the official notification before applying.

Syllabus

What the CDS Written Exam Covers

The written examination structure differs between OTA and the other three academies. IMA, INA and AFA candidates attempt three papers — English, General Knowledge and Elementary Mathematics. OTA candidates attempt only English and General Knowledge, without Mathematics.

English

Grammar and correct usage, vocabulary, sentence structure and improvement, reading comprehension, synonyms and antonyms, error identification, and ordering of words and sentences.

General Knowledge

Current events, History of India, Geography, General Science, Indian polity, economy and general awareness, and defence-related awareness — best built as a continuous, cumulative daily habit.

  • Elementary Mathematics (IMA / INA / AFA only) — arithmetic, algebra, geometry, mensuration, trigonometry, statistics and related school-level topics.
  • Not required for OTA — OTA's written exam has no Mathematics paper, making it comparatively accessible to non-mathematical backgrounds.
  • Objective format — all papers are objective-type, and subject to negative marking.
  • Two-hour papers — each paper is typically allotted about two hours in recent cycles.

Where CDS Leads

Careers After CDS

CDS is a pathway toward officer-level training, not an automatic guarantee of a commission — selected candidates proceed through academy training before progressing toward a commissioned officer role, subject to completing training and meeting all applicable service requirements.

Indian Army — IMA & OTA

Permanent commission (IMA) or Short Service Commission (OTA), with responsibilities centred on leadership, command and personnel management.

Indian Navy — INA

Commissioned officer roles combining specialised technical training with leadership responsibilities aboard naval assets.

Indian Air Force — AFA

Flying and non-flying branches depending on training performance and medical fitness — not every candidate becomes a pilot.

CDS vs NDA vs AFCAT

NDA is a Class-12-level entry route; CDS is graduate-level across all three services; AFCAT is Air-Force-specific and run by the IAF, not UPSC.

GICE Academy does not guarantee selection, SSB recommendation, rank, commission or any specific outcome. Preparation quality and consistency contribute to results, but outcomes depend on each candidate's performance across all official stages.

Common Questions

CDS Coaching in Thane — FAQ

What is the CDS Entrance Exam?

The Combined Defence Services Examination, conducted by UPSC to select graduate candidates for officer training at IMA, INA, AFA and OTA.

What is the CDS syllabus?

For IMA, INA and AFA: English, General Knowledge and Elementary Mathematics. For OTA: English and General Knowledge only.

Does OTA have Mathematics in the written exam?

No — OTA candidates attempt only English and General Knowledge, without an Elementary Mathematics paper.

Is there negative marking in CDS?

Yes, all papers are objective-type and subject to negative marking. The exact penalty should be verified from the current official notification.

What happens after the CDS written examination?

Candidates who clear the qualifying cut-off are called for the SSB interview, followed by a medical examination and final merit before academy allocation.

Does GICE Academy in Thane help with SSB preparation?

Yes — early SSB awareness covering officer-like qualities, communication and teamwork is built into the preparation journey alongside academic coaching for the written exam.
