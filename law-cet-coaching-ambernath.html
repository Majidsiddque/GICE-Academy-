About the Programme

LAW CET Preparation Built for Ambernath Students

MAH-LL.B. CET, conducted by the State Common Entrance Test Cell, Maharashtra, is the entrance examination that determines eligibility and merit rank for admission to law programmes at participating institutions across the state. Every year, students and graduates across Ambernath appear for it in the hope of securing a seat through the Centralised Admission Process (CAP).

The first step is understanding the right pathway: the 3-Year LLB CET is designed for graduates, while the 5-Year LLB CET is for students moving directly from Class 12 into an integrated law degree. Our Ambernath centre helps students identify the correct examination early, since eligibility, syllabus weightage and the admission process differ meaningfully between the two.

From there, preparation is built around legal reasoning, logical and analytical reasoning, English, and General Knowledge & Current Affairs — with Basic Mathematics added for 5-Year LLB CET aspirants — supported by previous-year question practice, timed mock tests, and consistent mentorship.

Which Exam Are You Targeting

3-Year LLB CET vs 5-Year LLB CET

MAH-LL.B. CET is conducted as two separate examinations, each leading to a different law programme, with its own application window, eligibility conditions and admission (CAP) process. A score from one cannot be used for admission through the other.

For Graduates
3-Year LLB CET

For candidates who hold, or are completing, a bachelor's degree in any discipline. Higher weightage on English and GK & Current Affairs in recent cycles, alongside Legal and Logical Reasoning.

For Class 12 Pass-Outs
5-Year Integrated LLB CET

For students who have completed, or are appearing for, Class 12. Legal Aptitude & Legal Reasoning and Logical & Analytical Reasoning typically carry the highest weightage, plus a Basic Mathematics (Class 10 level) section.

Exact eligibility percentages, question counts and section-wise weightage for each cycle are notified separately by the CET Cell. Always verify current figures in the latest official information brochure at cetcell.mahacet.org.

Who Can Apply

LAW CET Eligibility

Eligibility is programme-specific — the conditions for the 3-Year LLB CET differ from those for the 5-Year LLB CET.

3-Year LLB CET

A bachelor's degree (10+2+3 years or equivalent) in any discipline from a recognised university, or a postgraduate degree, with minimum qualifying marks that vary by category. Final-year graduation students may be permitted to apply provisionally, subject to the official notification.

5-Year LLB CET

Class 12 (10+2) pass, or equivalent, from a recognised board, with minimum qualifying marks that vary by category — commonly cited as approximately 45% for General/EWS in recent cycles, with relaxed thresholds for reserved categories. Students appearing for Class 12 boards may be permitted to apply, subject to conditions.

Age Limit

The age-limit position has been an evolving matter in recent years, linked to ongoing court proceedings before the Bombay High Court and Supreme Court. Do not assume a fixed age limit — check the current official notification before applying.

Reservation & Relaxations

Relaxed minimum qualifying marks and reserved seats apply for SC, ST, OBC, VJ/NT, SEBC, EWS, PwD and certain other categories during CAP, along with provisions for women and defence-quota candidates in several recent cycles, as per the official CAP brochure.

Minimum-mark percentages, age-limit provisions and reserved-category relaxations are revised by the CET Cell for every admission cycle. Always confirm against the current official MAH-LL.B. CET information brochure.

Syllabus

What the LAW CET Exam Covers

The syllabus is organised into a small number of broad sections common to both exams, though weightage differs between the 3-Year and 5-Year LLB CET.

Legal Aptitude & Legal Reasoning

Basic legal principles applied to hypothetical fact situations, legal awareness of the Indian legal system, judiciary and Constitution, common legal terminology, and elementary questions connected to contracts, torts and criminal law — tested at an aptitude level, not a law-school level.

Logical & Analytical Reasoning

Series, coding-decoding, blood relations, directions and distance, seating and other arrangements, syllogisms, statements and conclusions, analogies, classification, puzzles, and data-based reasoning.

General Knowledge & Current Affairs

Indian history, geography, polity and the Constitution, Indian economy, general science, national and international current events, awards, sports, important personalities, and government schemes.

  • English — reading comprehension, grammar, vocabulary, synonyms/antonyms, sentence correction, fill-in-the-blanks, idioms and general verbal ability.
  • Basic Mathematics (5-Year LLB CET only) — Class 10-level fundamentals, not part of the 3-Year LLB CET syllabus.
  • Weightage differs — 3-Year LLB CET leans more on English and GK; 5-Year LLB CET leans more on Legal and Logical Reasoning.
  • Objective format — computer-based, MCQ, generally with no negative marking as per recent notifications.

Where LAW CET Leads

Careers After a Law Degree

LAW CET is an entrance examination, not a professional qualification — after completing the corresponding law degree, students still need to fulfil Bar Council of India requirements, including enrolment and any applicable qualifying examinations such as the AIBE, before practising as an advocate.

Litigation & Corporate Practice

Advocate, legal associate at a law firm, corporate lawyer or in-house legal counsel, subject to Bar Council enrolment requirements.

Compliance & Consultancy

Legal consultant, compliance professional, and contract-management roles, often suited to 3-Year LLB graduates with a prior discipline background.

Government & Judiciary

Government legal roles and judicial services, after clearing the relevant judiciary examinations.

Research, Academia & Further Study

Legal research, legal academia, legal journalism, alternative dispute resolution, and further legal education such as an LL.M.

GICE Academy does not guarantee any specific rank, marks, admission, college seat, job or career outcome. The information above is meant to help students and parents understand the realistic scope of opportunities a law degree can open up.

Common Questions

LAW CET Coaching in Ambernath — FAQ

What is LAW CET?

LAW CET is the common entrance test used for admission to law programmes. In Maharashtra, it is officially conducted as MAH-LL.B. CET by the State Common Entrance Test Cell, Maharashtra.

What is the difference between 3-Year and 5-Year LLB CET?

They are separate examinations with different eligibility (graduate vs. Class 12 pass), different syllabus weightage, and separate CAP admission processes — the 5-Year CET also typically includes a Basic Mathematics section.

What is the LAW CET syllabus?

It generally covers Legal Aptitude & Legal Reasoning, Logical & Analytical Reasoning, General Knowledge & Current Affairs, and English — with Basic Mathematics added for the 5-Year LLB CET in recent cycles.

Is there negative marking in LAW CET?

As per recent official notifications, there is no negative marking — but always verify this against the current year's marking scheme before the exam.

Is there an age limit for LAW CET?

The age-limit position has changed across recent cycles and has been linked to ongoing court proceedings. Always check the current official notification rather than assuming a fixed age limit.

Does GICE Academy in Ambernath help with LAW CET mock tests?

Yes — subject-wise practice, previous-year question practice, sectional tests and full-length mock tests with detailed performance analysis are built into the preparation journey.
