About the Programme

CMAT Preparation Built for Dombivli Aspirants

CMAT (Common Management Admission Test) is a national-level, computer-based entrance examination conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) for admission to MBA and PGDM programmes. CMAT scores are accepted by AICTE-approved institutions, university departments, constituent colleges, and affiliated colleges across India.

CMAT evaluates quantitative ability, logical reasoning, language comprehension, general awareness, and business and entrepreneurship concepts, all within a strict three-hour window. Success depends less on last-minute cramming and more on structured, sustained preparation.

Our Dombivli centre supports CMAT aspirants through concept building, guided practice, CMAT-style mock tests, detailed performance analysis, and personalised faculty mentorship, following a simple philosophy: Learn → Practise → Test → Analyse → Improve → Repeat.

Exam Structure

The Five CMAT Sections

CMAT-2026 is organised into five sections of 20 questions each, all within a single, un-sectioned 180-minute window.

Who Can Apply

CMAT Eligibility Criteria

Based on the official CMAT Information Bulletin published by NTA, eligibility to appear for CMAT is kept broad.

Educational Qualification

Candidates must hold a Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognised university.

Final-Year Students

Candidates in their final year of a Bachelor's Degree are also eligible to apply, provided their result is declared before the commencement of admission for the relevant academic session.

Nationality & Age

The candidate must be a citizen of India. There is no upper age restriction for appearing in CMAT.

CMAT Eligibility vs College Eligibility

Meeting CMAT's basic eligibility allows you to sit for the exam, but each institute sets its own additional requirements for admission — minimum graduation marks, subject background, or work-experience preferences.

Always verify the specific admission eligibility criteria of your target institutes directly on their official websites, since these can vary from what is required merely to sit for CMAT.

Syllabus

What the CMAT Sections Cover

The CMAT syllabus spans five sections. Here is a section-wise breakdown of the topics most relevant to preparation.

Quantitative Techniques & Data Interpretation

Number system, arithmetic, percentages, profit and loss, ratio and proportion, averages, time and work, time-speed-distance, algebra, geometry, mensuration, probability, permutation-combination, and data interpretation from tables, charts and graphs.

Logical Reasoning

Coding-decoding, number and letter series, blood relations, direction sense, seating arrangements and puzzles, syllogisms, analogy and classification, and analytical/data-based reasoning.

  • Language Comprehension — reading comprehension, vocabulary, grammar and sentence correction, fill in the blanks, para jumbles, and inference/tone-based questions.
  • General Awareness — current affairs (national, international, business, appointments, sports, science) and static GK (history, geography, polity, economy, organisations).
  • Innovation & Entrepreneurship — entrepreneurship concepts, business fundamentals, innovation and business models, startups, business terminology, and related government initiatives.
  • Sustained awareness — General Awareness and Innovation & Entrepreneurship reward consistent, ongoing revision rather than last-minute cramming.

Common Questions

CMAT Coaching in Dombivli — FAQ

What is the CMAT exam?

CMAT (Common Management Admission Test) is a national-level, computer-based entrance examination conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) for admission to MBA/PGDM programmes at AICTE-approved institutes across India.

What is the CMAT exam pattern?

CMAT-2026 has 100 questions across five sections (20 each), for a total of 400 marks, with +4 for every correct answer and -1 for every incorrect answer, in a 180-minute computer-based test.

What is the CMAT eligibility criteria?

Candidates must hold a Bachelor's Degree in any discipline (final-year students can also apply, subject to conditions) and be a citizen of India. There is no upper age limit.

What is CMAT percentile?

CMAT percentile reflects a candidate's performance relative to other candidates who appeared for the exam (or the relevant shift), rather than the raw percentage of marks scored.

Which colleges accept CMAT scores?

CMAT scores are accepted by many AICTE-approved management institutes, university departments, constituent colleges and affiliated colleges across India. Always verify current cut-offs on official sources.

Does GICE Academy in Dombivli help with CMAT mock tests and sectional practice?

Yes — sectional tests, topic tests and full-length mock tests simulating the real 180-minute format, along with detailed performance analysis, are built into the preparation journey.
