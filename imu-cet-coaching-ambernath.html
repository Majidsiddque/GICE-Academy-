About the Programme

IMU CET Preparation Built for Ambernath Aspirants

IMU CET — the Indian Maritime University Common Entrance Test — is the national-level, computer-based gateway examination that decides admission to undergraduate and postgraduate maritime programmes offered by the Indian Maritime University and its DG Shipping-approved affiliated institutes across India. IMU itself is a central university functioning under the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways, Government of India, offering specialised education in nautical science, marine engineering, naval architecture and ocean engineering, and maritime management.

Because IMU CET is not one uniform test — eligibility, subjects, and even exam pattern differ across the undergraduate and postgraduate streams — our Ambernath centre starts by helping each candidate confirm their target programme first, since that decision shapes everything that follows in their preparation.

From there, preparation combines syllabus-based teaching across Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, English and General Aptitude, regular previous-year question practice, and full-length mock tests formatted to the real exam, together with the mentorship and doubt-solving support a technical, high-stakes entrance exam like this genuinely needs.

Who Can Apply

IMU CET Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility is programme-specific — UG and PG requirements differ meaningfully, so always confirm exact figures against the latest official notification.

UG Academic Qualification

Class 12 (10+2) pass or appearing, with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, scoring at least 60% aggregate in PCM and 50% in English (SC/ST relaxation of 5% typically applies to PCM marks only).

Age Limit

A programme-specific upper age limit applies for UG programmes, with relaxation for OBC(NCL)/SC/ST candidates — always verify current limits officially, as age-limit norms for maritime programmes have historically been stricter than for many other entrance exams.

Medical & Physical Fitness

Candidates for seagoing UG programmes are typically required to meet DG Shipping medical fitness standards, including normal colour vision and adequate binocular vision, assessed by an approved medical practitioner.

PG Eligibility

A relevant bachelor's/engineering degree with a minimum aggregate (commonly around 60%, with SC/ST relaxation as officially notified) — exact requirements vary by the specific PG programme.

GICE Academy does not provide medical advice or medical clearance. Eligibility conditions differ by programme and are revised with every admission cycle — always verify current details from the official IMU CET notification and, where relevant, consult an approved medical examiner well before applying.

Syllabus

What the IMU CET UG Exam Covers

The syllabus for undergraduate programmes is drawn broadly from the Class 11 and Class 12 curriculum in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, along with English language skills and General Knowledge/Aptitude. Treat the breakdown below as a preparation guide and check it against the latest official syllabus.

Mathematics

Relations & Functions, Inverse Trigonometric Functions, Matrices, Determinants, Continuity & Differentiability, Applications of Derivatives, Integrals & their Applications, Differential Equations, Vectors, Three-Dimensional Geometry, Linear Programming, Probability.

Physics

Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetic Effects of Current & Magnetism, Electromagnetic Induction & Alternating Currents, Electromagnetic Waves, Optics (Ray & Wave), Dual Nature of Radiation & Matter, Atoms & Nuclei, Semiconductor Electronics.

  • Chemistry — Physical (Solutions, Electrochemistry, Chemical Kinetics, Surface Chemistry), Inorganic (p-Block, d- & f-Block Elements, Coordination Compounds), and Organic (Haloalkanes, Alcohols/Phenols/Ethers, Aldehydes/Ketones/Carboxylic Acids, Biomolecules, Polymers).
  • English — Parts of Speech, Synonyms & Antonyms, Tenses, Phrasal Verbs, Idioms, Sentence Correction & Error Detection, Reading Comprehension.
  • General Aptitude (where included) — General Knowledge & Current Affairs, Logical & Critical Reasoning, Data Interpretation & Sufficiency, Numerical Computation.
  • Quantitative Topics — Time & Work, Time & Distance, Boats & Streams, Ratio & Mixture, Clocks & Calendars.

Where IMU CET Leads

Careers After IMU CET

IMU CET itself does not provide a career or a job — clearing it only provides entry into a maritime education programme. What follows academic education, and for many programmes, mandatory training, sea service and statutory certification, is what actually builds eligibility for maritime career pathways.

Marine Engineering Pathway

Shipboard engineering roles and shore-based maritime technical roles, subject to further training, certification and sea service.

Nautical / Deck Officer Pathway

Deck-department and officer-track roles, progressing through statutory certification defined by maritime authorities.

Naval Architecture & Ocean Engineering

Shipbuilding and design roles, structural engineering, the offshore sector, and maritime engineering consulting.

Maritime Management

Port, shipping and logistics management roles through MBA Port & Shipping and related PG programmes.

GICE Academy does not guarantee employment, placement, rank, admission, or salary outcomes of any kind. Career progression in maritime professions typically requires additional training, statutory certification, ongoing medical fitness, and mandatory sea service where applicable — verify all current requirements through official maritime authorities and IMU.

Common Questions

IMU CET Coaching in Ambernath — FAQ

What is IMU CET?

IMU CET is the Indian Maritime University Common Entrance Test — a national-level, computer-based exam for admission to maritime programmes offered by IMU and its affiliated institutes.

What is the eligibility for IMU CET (UG)?

Candidates must have passed or be appearing for Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, scoring at least 60% aggregate in PCM and 50% in English, along with a programme-specific age limit.

What is the IMU CET exam pattern?

A computer-based test with 200 questions across Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, English and General Knowledge/Aptitude, conducted over 3 hours, with negative marking as specified in the official notification.

Are there medical or physical fitness requirements?

Yes — candidates for seagoing programmes are typically required to meet DG Shipping medical fitness standards, including colour vision and binocular vision checks by an approved medical examiner.

What programmes can I apply for through IMU CET?

Depending on the current admission cycle, programmes may include B.Tech Marine Engineering, B.Tech Naval Architecture & Ocean Engineering, B.Sc. Nautical Science, DNS, and PG programmes like M.Tech and MBA Port and Shipping Management.

Does clearing IMU CET guarantee a Merchant Navy job?

No. IMU CET provides entry into a maritime education programme only — further mandatory training, sea service and statutory certification are required, and no employment outcome is guaranteed.

Do you offer online classes for Ambernath students?

Yes — online batches run alongside our in-person sessions, with the same study material, mock test access, and faculty availability for doubts.

Does GICE Academy in Ambernath provide mock tests and mentorship?

Yes — structured mock tests, previous-year question-paper practice, detailed performance analysis, and ongoing faculty mentorship are built into the preparation journey.
