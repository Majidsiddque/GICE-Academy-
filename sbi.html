About State Bank of India

State Bank of India is one of India's major public sector banking institutions. SBI provides banking and financial services through an extensive network across India and internationally.

SBI conducts recruitment examinations for several positions, including Probationary Officers, Junior Associates and Specialist Officers.

Preparation for SBI examinations requires a balanced approach covering concepts, question practice, speed, accuracy, current affairs and examination strategy.

SBI Examination Preparation

SBI Probationary Officer

Preparation for the SBI PO recruitment process covering preliminary examination, main examination and the final selection stages.

SBI Clerk

Focused preparation for Junior Associate recruitment with emphasis on English, reasoning, numerical ability and awareness.

SBI Specialist Officer

Preparation for specialised banking positions where candidates are assessed on professional knowledge along with applicable aptitude areas.

SBI Probationary Officer (PO)

SBI PO is a graduate-level recruitment examination for a managerial entry position in State Bank of India. The recruitment process is conducted through multiple stages.

Candidates generally need to develop strong skills in English, quantitative aptitude, reasoning, data analysis, banking awareness and descriptive writing.

Preparation Focus

Concept clarity, sectional practice, mock tests, current affairs, banking awareness, descriptive writing and interview preparation.

SBI PO Selection Process

1

Preliminary Exam

The first examination stage used for shortlisting candidates.

2

Main Exam

A broader assessment covering objective and descriptive components.

3

Interview / Final Stage

Shortlisted candidates proceed to the applicable final assessment stage.

SBI Clerk – Junior Associate

SBI Clerk recruitment is conducted for Junior Associate positions. The role involves customer service and routine banking operations.

Preparation commonly focuses on English language, numerical ability, reasoning ability, general and financial awareness, and computer-related topics where applicable.

Preliminary Examination

Practice English, numerical ability and reasoning questions with an emphasis on speed and accuracy.

Main Examination

Broader preparation covering awareness, English, quantitative aptitude, reasoning and computer aptitude.

Language Proficiency

Candidates may need to satisfy the applicable local language requirements for the recruitment process.

SBI Specialist Officer (SO)

SBI Specialist Officer recruitment is intended for candidates applying for specialised professional roles within the bank.

Depending on the position, specialist roles can involve areas such as information technology, finance, law, human resources, marketing and risk-related functions.

Professional Knowledge

Specialist Officer preparation should combine professional or domain knowledge with the reasoning, quantitative and English components specified for the particular recruitment.

SBI Exam Preparation Strategy

Build Concepts

Strengthen fundamental concepts before moving towards advanced and time-bound question practice.

Practice Regularly

Solve sectional and full-length tests regularly to improve accuracy and familiarity with different question types.

Analyse Performance

Review mock-test performance to identify weak areas, repeated mistakes and topics requiring additional practice.

Important Areas for SBI Preparation

  • Quantitative Aptitude and Numerical Ability
  • Reasoning Ability and Logical Reasoning
  • English Language and Reading Comprehension
  • Current Affairs and General Awareness
  • Banking and Financial Awareness
  • Data Interpretation and Analysis
  • Computer Aptitude where applicable
  • Descriptive Writing for applicable examinations
  • Professional Knowledge for Specialist Officer posts
