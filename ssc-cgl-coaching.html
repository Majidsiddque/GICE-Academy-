About the Programme

SSC CGL Preparation Built Around the Real Exam

SSC CGL is a national-level examination conducted by the Staff Selection Commission to recruit graduates for Group B and Group C posts across Central Government ministries, departments and organisations — open to graduates from any discipline, which is why it attracts one of the largest and most diverse applicant pools among Indian competitive exams.

The exam follows a two-stage, fully computer-based structure — Tier-I and Tier-II — with no separate descriptive paper or interview. Our coaching is built around that actual structure: Tier-I as the qualifying, shortlisting stage, and Tier-II, where final merit is determined, prepared for from day one rather than postponed until after Tier-I results.

Preparation follows one consistent cycle: Learn → Practise → Test → Analyse → Improve → Repeat, with speed and accuracy trained together under timed, exam-like mock conditions.

Who Can Apply

SSC CGL Eligibility

Because SSC CGL recruits for many different posts through one exam, eligibility is not identical for every post — candidates must check requirements for each post they intend to apply for.

Educational Qualification

A bachelor's degree from a recognised university, open to any discipline for most posts. Posts like Junior Statistical Officer and Statistical Investigator Grade-II typically require Mathematics/Statistics/Economics at a specified level. Final-year students may apply provisionally.

Age Limit

Not uniform across posts — different posts are grouped under different age brackets, commonly ranging between the late teens and early thirties, as specified in the current notification. Age relaxation applies for SC/ST/OBC/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen categories.

Physical & Medical (Select Posts)

Enforcement and field-oriented posts — Inspector (Central Excise/Examiner/Preventive Officer), Sub-Inspector posts — carry additional height, chest and medical fitness standards that differ by post and gender category.

Because eligibility genuinely differs from post to post, always cross-check the specific qualification, age bracket and any post-specific requirements using the latest official SSC CGL notification at ssc.gov.in.

Syllabus

SSC CGL Syllabus Subject-Wise

  • General Intelligence & Reasoning — analogy, coding-decoding, syllogism, blood relations, non-verbal reasoning (mirror/water images, paper folding, embedded figures).
  • General Awareness — current affairs, history, geography, polity, economy, general science, static GK, government schemes.
  • Quantitative Aptitude — number system, percentage, ratio, SI/CI, time & work, algebra, geometry, mensuration, trigonometry, data interpretation.
  • English Language & Comprehension — vocabulary, idioms, error spotting, cloze test, para jumbles, active/passive voice, reading comprehension.

Tier-II Specific

Paper-I (compulsory for all): Mathematical Abilities, Reasoning & General Intelligence, English Language & Comprehension, General Awareness, Computer Knowledge Test, Data Entry Speed Test. Paper-II (Statistics) applies only to JSO and Statistical Investigator Grade-II aspirants. Paper-III (Finance & Economics) applies only to Assistant Audit/Accounts Officer aspirants.

Paper-II and Paper-III are optional and post-specific — not compulsory for every candidate. The exact syllabus and any revisions should always be verified against the latest official notification.

Exam Pattern

SSC CGL Tier-I & Tier-II Pattern

SSC CGL follows a two-stage, fully computer-based structure. Tier-I is qualifying and shortlisting only; Tier-II performance determines final merit and post allocation.

Tier-I & Tier-II — Broad Structure
StageModeBroad ComponentsPurpose

Tier-IIComputer-Based (CBT)Paper-I (compulsory); Paper-II (Statistics, specific posts); Paper-III (Finance & Economics, specific posts)Main exam; determines final merit
  • Negative marking — applies in both Tier-I and Tier-II for incorrect answers.
  • Qualifying components — Computer Knowledge Test and Data Entry Speed Test are generally qualifying, though some posts require a higher standard.
  • No interview — the current structure has no separate descriptive Tier-III paper or personal interview.
  • Four tiers, replaced — this two-tier structure replaced the earlier four-tier format.

The precise number of questions, marks, timing and negative-marking values for Tier-I and each Tier-II paper are revised periodically. Always confirm exact figures from the latest official SSC CGL notification.

Common Questions

SSC CGL Coaching — FAQ

What is the SSC CGL exam?

A national-level exam conducted by the Staff Selection Commission to recruit graduates for Group B and Group C posts across Central Government ministries and departments.

What is the current SSC CGL exam pattern?

A two-tier, fully computer-based pattern — Tier-I (qualifying) and Tier-II (scoring, with Paper-I compulsory and Paper-II/III for specific posts). There is no separate descriptive paper or interview.

Do Tier-I marks count toward final merit?

No — Tier-I is qualifying and shortlisting only. Tier-II performance forms the basis of the final merit list.

Who needs to attempt Paper-II or Paper-III?

Only candidates applying for posts linked to Statistics (like Junior Statistical Officer) or Finance & Economics (like Assistant Audit/Accounts Officer) — they are not compulsory for every candidate.

Can final-year graduates apply?

Yes, generally provisionally — but proof of having completed the degree must be produced by the date specified for document verification.

Does GICE Academy prepare students for Tier-II from the start?

Yes — Tier-II preparation begins early alongside Tier-I rather than being postponed until after results, covering Mathematical Abilities, Reasoning, English, GA, Computer Knowledge and post-specific papers where relevant.
