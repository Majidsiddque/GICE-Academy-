What is the SSC CGL Examination?

The Staff Selection Commission Combined Graduate Level examination, commonly known as SSC CGL, is conducted for recruitment to various Group B and Group C positions in central government departments and organisations.

The examination is primarily intended for candidates with a bachelor's degree, subject to the specific educational, age and post-wise requirements mentioned in the applicable SSC notification.

    Eligibility

    Candidates should carefully check the latest SSC recruitment notification for the exact eligibility requirements applicable to the recruitment cycle.

  • A bachelor's degree from a recognised university or equivalent is generally required.
  • Age limits vary according to the post and applicable recruitment rules.
  • Certain posts may have additional educational, physical or other eligibility requirements.
  • Candidates should verify post-specific conditions before applying.

    SSC CGL Preparation

  • Build strong fundamentals across the prescribed subjects.
  • Practise examination-level questions regularly.
  • Use sectional tests to identify strengths and areas requiring additional preparation.
  • Attempt full-length mock tests to practise time management.
  • Revise important concepts and maintain concise revision notes.
  • Analyse mock-test performance and work on recurring mistakes.

SSC CGL Exam Pattern

General Intelligence & Reasoning

Logical reasoning, analytical ability, classification, analogy and other reasoning topics prescribed for the examination.

General Awareness

Current affairs and general knowledge covering areas such as history, geography, polity, economy and general science.

Quantitative Aptitude

Arithmetic and mathematical concepts required for competitive examination preparation.

English Comprehension

Grammar, vocabulary, comprehension and language-based questions.

SSC CGL Tier I Examination

General Intelligence & Reasoning

25 Questions — 50 Marks

General Awareness

25 Questions — 50 Marks

Quantitative Aptitude

25 Questions — 50 Marks

English Comprehension

25 Questions — 50 Marks

Total: 100 Questions and 200 Marks. Examination patterns, marking schemes and other requirements can change between recruitment cycles. Candidates should verify the current official SSC notification before applying.

SSC CGL Selection Process

  • Tier I — Computer-based objective examination.
  • Tier II — Subsequent computer-based examination involving the papers and modules applicable to the recruitment.
  • Document Verification — Candidates shortlisted according to the applicable process undergo document verification.

Regular Practice

Solve questions regularly to improve familiarity with different question types.

Revision

Mock Tests
