SSC MTS (Multi-Tasking Staff)

The SSC MTS examination is conducted by the Staff Selection Commission for recruitment to various non-technical Group C positions in different departments and offices of the Government of India. The examination is an opportunity for candidates who have completed their matriculation-level education and want to pursue a career in the central government sector.

  • Exam: Staff Selection Commission MTS
  • Qualification: 10th Pass
  • Job Type: Central Government
  • Selection: Computer-Based Examination

Important: The exact eligibility criteria, vacancies, examination structure and dates can vary by recruitment cycle. Candidates should always check the latest official SSC notification.

Subjects for SSC MTS Preparation

Build your preparation around the major areas tested in the computer-based examination.

Numerical & Mathematical Ability

Arithmetic fundamentals, percentages, averages, ratios, time and work, profit and loss, and other basic concepts.

Reasoning Ability

Patterns, logical relationships, series, classification, coding-decoding and problem-solving questions.

General Awareness

Current affairs, general science, history, geography, polity and other relevant areas.

English Language

Grammar, vocabulary, comprehension and basic English language skills.

SSC MTS Eligibility

  • Educational Qualification: Candidates generally need to have passed matriculation or the equivalent qualification from a recognized board.
  • Age Limit: The applicable age limit depends on the recruitment notification and the post. Age relaxations are available for eligible categories according to government rules.
Always verify: Educational qualification, age limits, nationality requirements, vacancies and category-wise relaxations from the latest official notification before submitting an application.

SSC MTS Preparation Strategy

A consistent preparation routine can help you build speed, accuracy and confidence.

01. Build Fundamentals

Start with basic concepts in mathematics, reasoning, English and general awareness.

02. Practice Daily

Solve topic-wise questions regularly and keep track of mistakes.

03. Attempt Mock Tests

Take timed mock tests to improve your speed, accuracy and time management.

04. Revise Regularly

Maintain short revision notes and regularly revise important concepts and current affairs.

05. Analyse Mistakes

Review incorrect answers and identify the topics where additional practice is required.

06. Follow the Notification

Keep yourself updated with the latest SSC notification, schedule and syllabus.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SSC MTS?
SSC MTS stands for Staff Selection Commission Multi-Tasking Staff. It is a recruitment examination for various Group C positions in central government departments.
What qualification is required for SSC MTS?
The recruitment generally requires candidates to have passed matriculation or an equivalent qualification from a recognized board.
Is SSC MTS conducted every year?
SSC conducts MTS recruitment according to its recruitment schedule. Candidates should check the latest SSC notification for the current cycle.
What subjects should I prepare?
Preparation generally covers numerical and mathematical ability, reasoning and problem solving, general awareness and English language and comprehension.
How can I prepare for SSC MTS?
Build your fundamentals, practise questions regularly, revise important topics and take timed mock tests to improve speed and accuracy.
