Which RRB Exam?

Major RRB Exams at a Glance

Identifying the right exam for your qualification and career interest is the essential first step — eligibility, syllabus and selection stages differ meaningfully across all of these.

Non-Technical
RRB NTPC

Station Master, Goods Train Manager, Traffic Assistant, Commercial cum Ticket Clerk and other administrative/operational posts.

Eligibility: 12th pass (Undergraduate level) or Graduate, as per post.

Stages: CBT 1, CBT 2, Typing Skill Test/CBAT (post-dependent), DV, Medical.

Entry Grade
RRB Group D (Level-1)

Track Maintainer, Pointsman, Helper/Assistant and similar posts — the largest RRB recruitment by applicant volume.

Eligibility: 10th pass or ITI/National Apprenticeship Certificate.

Stages: Single CBT, Physical Efficiency Test (PET), DV, Medical.

Technical
RRB ALP

Assistant Loco Pilot — operating and assisting with train movement, requiring specific medical and vision standards.

Eligibility: 10th pass with ITI, or relevant Diploma/Degree in Engineering.

Stages: CBT 1, CBT 2 (Part A & B), CBAT, DV, Medical.

Technical
RRB Technician

Technician Grade I (Signal) and Grade III posts across various technical trades.

Eligibility: 10+2 with Physics & Maths, ITI, or relevant Diploma/Degree, grade-dependent.

Stages: Single CBT, DV, Medical — no separate aptitude test.

Technical / Supervisory
RRB JE

Junior Engineer, Depot Material Superintendent, Chemical & Metallurgical Assistant.

Eligibility: Diploma or Degree in the relevant Engineering discipline.

Stages: CBT 1, CBT 2, DV, Medical.

Other
Other RRB Recruitments

Paramedical categories, Ministerial and Isolated Categories, Security posts, notified as and when vacancies arise.

Note: Eligibility, syllabus and stages announced separately for each cycle.

Vacancy numbers, exact eligibility and selection stages change with every recruitment cycle. Always confirm details against the latest official RRB notification (CEN) before applying.

Who Can Apply

RRB Eligibility & Age Limit

Educational Qualification

Varies by exam: 12th pass or Graduation for NTPC (level-dependent); 10th pass or ITI for Group D; 10th with ITI or Engineering Diploma/Degree for ALP; 10+2 with Physics & Maths, ITI, or Diploma/Degree for Technician; Diploma/Degree in Engineering for JE.

Age Limit (Indicative, General Category)

Recent 2026-cycle notifications indicate 18–33 years for Group D and JE, 18–30 years for ALP and Technician Grade III, 18–33 years for Technician Grade I, and 18–33 or 18–36 years for NTPC depending on level. Standard relaxation applies for OBC-NCL, SC/ST, PwBD and other notified categories.

Nationality

Generally Indian citizens, with certain other categories eligible as specified in the official notification.

Age limits, cut-off dates and category-wise relaxations can change with every recruitment cycle. The figures above reflect recent notifications at the time of writing — re-verify against the current official RRB notification before applying.

Syllabus

RRB Syllabus Overview

Most RRB Computer-Based Tests share a common core, with technical examinations adding post-specific technical or trade subjects on top.

  • Mathematics — number system, percentage, ratio, SI/CI, time & work, mensuration, algebra, trigonometry, data interpretation.
  • General Intelligence & Reasoning — analogies, coding-decoding, series, syllogism, blood relations, puzzle-based questions.
  • General Awareness & Current Affairs — national/international events, polity, economy, geography, sports, railway-related developments.
  • General Science — Physics, Chemistry and Biology fundamentals, at a level matched to the target exam.

Technical & Professional Knowledge (ALP, Technician, JE)

Engineering fundamentals relevant to the candidate's trade or discipline, electrical and mechanical concepts, electronics, and trade-specific subjects for ITI-linked posts — as prescribed in the relevant official notification.

There is no single, universal syllabus for all RRB exams. Always download and follow the syllabus PDF published with the relevant official RRB notification for your target exam.

Common Questions

RRB Coaching in Dombivli — FAQ

What is the RRB Entrance Exam?

An umbrella term for several distinct examinations conducted by Railway Recruitment Boards — including NTPC, Group D, ALP, Technician and JE — each with its own eligibility, syllabus and pattern.

Which RRB exam should I prepare for?

It depends on your qualification: 10th pass/ITI for Group D, 12th pass/Graduate for NTPC, technical ITI/Diploma/Degree for ALP, Technician and JE. Identifying the right exam first is the essential first step.

Is there a common RRB syllabus?

No single universal syllabus exists, but Mathematics, Reasoning, General Awareness and General Science are common across most exams, with technical exams adding trade or engineering subjects.

Does Group D include a physical test?

Yes — Group D includes a Physical Efficiency Test (PET) in addition to the CBT, unlike NTPC, Technician or JE.

Is there negative marking in RRB exams?

Yes, in most RRB exams — for example, 1/3 mark per wrong answer in Group D and ALP CBTs. Exact figures should be verified from the current official notification.

Does GICE Academy in Dombivli help with technical subjects for ALP, Technician and JE?

Yes — dedicated coaching in engineering fundamentals and trade-specific content is built in for candidates targeting these technical posts, alongside the common subjects.
