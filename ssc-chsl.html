SSC Examination

What is the SSC CHSL Examination?

The Staff Selection Commission Combined Higher Secondary Level examination is conducted for recruitment to various posts in government departments and offices.

SSC CHSL is designed for candidates who meet the educational and other requirements specified in the relevant recruitment notification.

What Does SSC CHSL Preparation Require?

Preparation involves building fundamentals, practising objective questions, improving speed and accuracy, revising regularly and becoming familiar with the examination format.

SSC CHSL Preparation

Concept Building

Develop a strong foundation in mathematics, reasoning, English and general awareness.

Regular Practice

Solve questions regularly to improve accuracy and familiarity with different question patterns.

Mock Tests

Use timed mock tests to practise managing questions within the examination environment.

SSC CHSL Selection Process

Tier I

Computer-based objective examination covering the prescribed subjects.

Tier II

The next stage evaluates candidates through the components specified in the applicable examination scheme.

Skill / Typing Test

Depending on the post, candidates may have to qualify in the applicable skill or typing assessment.

SSC CHSL Tier I Exam Pattern

Subject Questions Marks
General Intelligence 25 50
General Awareness 25 50
Quantitative Aptitude 25 50
English Language 25 50
Total 100 200

Examination patterns and requirements may change between recruitment cycles. Candidates should always verify the latest official notification.

SSC CHSL Subjects

General Intelligence

Logical reasoning and analytical ability questions.

General Awareness

General knowledge and current affairs relevant to the examination.

Quantitative Aptitude

Numerical and mathematical ability questions.

English Language

Grammar, vocabulary, comprehension and language-based questions.

SSC CHSL Eligibility

  • Educational Qualification: Candidates generally need to have passed the required higher-secondary level qualification, subject to the specific post and notification.
  • Age Requirement: The applicable age range is specified in the recruitment notification and may vary according to the relevant rules.
  • Post Requirements: Some posts can have additional educational, skill or other requirements.

How to Prepare for SSC CHSL

1. Build Your Basics

Start with the fundamental concepts of each subject before moving to more challenging questions.

2. Practise Daily

Consistent practice can help improve accuracy, speed and familiarity with examination questions.

3. Revise Regularly

Maintain revision notes and revisit important formulas, concepts and facts.

4. Take Mock Tests

Attempt timed tests and review mistakes after every test to identify areas requiring further preparation.

Practice SSC CHSL Mock Tests

Use practice tests to become familiar with question formats, practise time management and review your performance.
