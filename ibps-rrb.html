About the Examination

About IBPS RRB

IBPS RRB examinations are conducted for recruitment to various posts in Regional Rural Banks.

The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection conducts the Common Recruitment Process for Regional Rural Banks. The recruitment process covers different posts and stages depending on the position.

Preparation generally requires a strong command of quantitative aptitude, reasoning, English or Hindi language, general awareness, computer knowledge and banking or financial awareness, as applicable to the examination.

Choose Your Target

IBPS RRB Exam Types

Officer Scale-I

Preparation for the Officer Scale-I recruitment process, including the applicable preliminary, main and subsequent selection stages.

Specialist Officer

Specialist Officer recruitment covers professional streams such as IT, Agriculture, Law, Treasury, Marketing and other notified disciplines.

Office Assistant

Preparation for the Office Assistant recruitment process with emphasis on speed, accuracy and objective-question practice.

Officer Scale-I Prelims

  • Reasoning
  • Quantitative Aptitude
  • Speed and accuracy practice
  • Section-wise and full-length mock tests

Officer Scale-I Mains

  • Reasoning and Computer Knowledge
  • Quantitative Aptitude / Data Interpretation
  • General Awareness and Banking Awareness
  • English or Hindi Language, as applicable
Selection

Officer Scale-I Selection Process

1

Prelims

Objective preliminary examination for initial shortlisting.

2

Mains

Broader objective examination covering the notified subjects.

3

Interview

Applicable to Officer Scale-I candidates who qualify the required stages.

4

Final Selection

Selection is based on the applicable recruitment rules and official notification.

Specialist Recruitment

IBPS RRB Specialist Officer

Specialist Officer recruitment is intended for candidates applying to professional roles notified by participating Regional Rural Banks. The exact disciplines and eligibility requirements depend on the recruitment cycle.

Professional Knowledge

Build strong command over the professional subject relevant to the post and practise exam-oriented questions.

Reasoning & Aptitude

Strengthen logical reasoning, numerical aptitude and other common sections included in the applicable pattern.

Mock Practice

Use timed tests to improve accuracy, identify weak areas and develop an effective attempt strategy.

Office Assistant

IBPS RRB Office Assistant

Office Assistant preparation focuses heavily on speed and accuracy because candidates must solve a large number of objective questions within a limited time.

Preliminary Focus

  • Reasoning
  • Numerical Ability
  • Speed-building practice
  • Sectional and full mocks

Main Exam Focus

  • Reasoning
  • Numerical Ability
  • General Awareness
  • Language and Computer Knowledge as applicable
What To Study

Important IBPS RRB Topics

Quantitative AptitudeArithmetic, simplification, number series, data interpretation, percentages, ratios and related topics.
ReasoningPuzzles, seating arrangements, syllogism, inequalities, coding-decoding and logical reasoning.
LanguageGrammar, vocabulary, reading comprehension, error detection and other notified language topics.
Banking & General AwarenessBanking awareness, current affairs, financial awareness and important national and international developments.
Computer KnowledgeComputer fundamentals, operating systems, internet, networking and common applications.
Data InterpretationTables, charts, graphs and caselet-based calculations with emphasis on speed and accuracy.
How To Prepare

IBPS RRB Preparation Strategy

1. Build Concepts

Start with fundamentals and create a clear base in arithmetic, reasoning, language and banking awareness.

2. Practise Daily

Use topic-wise questions to improve calculation speed, logical thinking and accuracy.

3. Analyse Mocks

Attempt timed mock tests and review every error to identify recurring weaknesses.

Focus On Accuracy

Do not chase attempts at the expense of accuracy. Track your performance by topic and gradually improve your solving speed.

Revise Regularly

Maintain short revision notes for formulas, vocabulary, banking awareness and frequently repeated concepts.
