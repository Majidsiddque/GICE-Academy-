About the Programme

SNAP Preparation Built for Dombivli Aspirants

SNAP stands for the Symbiosis National Aptitude Test, a national-level management entrance examination conducted by Symbiosis International (Deemed University), Pune (SIU), for admission to its MBA and other management programmes offered through its constituent Symbiosis institutes.

SNAP is a short, fast-paced, computer-based test that assesses General English, Analytical & Logical Reasoning, and Quantitative Ability in just 60 minutes. It is one of the most-attempted MBA entrance exams in India after CAT, largely because it opens the door to the well-established Symbiosis network of institutes.

Our Dombivli centre helps SNAP aspirants build strong fundamentals, practise with purpose, and take realistic mock tests, supported by personalised faculty guidance and mentorship, following a simple cycle: Practice → Test → Analyse → Correct → Practise Again → Improve.

Exam Structure

The Three SNAP Sections

SNAP tests three broad skill areas within a single 60-minute window, with no separate time limit per section — candidates can move between sections freely.

Multi-Session
Computer-Based Format

SNAP is a fully computer-based test, typically conducted across multiple sessions in the same admission cycle, with the best score used for percentile calculation.

Exact question counts, sectional weightage, marking scheme and number of sessions are set by Symbiosis International (Deemed University) and can change from year to year. Always verify current details on snaptest.org before finalising your preparation plan.

Who Can Apply

SNAP Eligibility Criteria

It is important to distinguish between the basic eligibility required to appear for SNAP and the eligibility required for admission to a specific Symbiosis programme the two are not the same.

Educational Qualification

A bachelor's degree of at least three years, in any discipline, from a recognised university, with a minimum aggregate of 50% for General category candidates (45% for SC/ST/PwD candidates).

Final-Year Students

Candidates in their final year of graduation may also apply; admission remains provisional, subject to proof of successful degree completion within the specified timeline.

Age & Foreign Degrees

There is no upper age limit prescribed for appearing in SNAP. Candidates with a foreign university degree generally require an equivalence certificate from the Association of Indian Universities (AIU).

Programme-Specific Requirements

Beyond basic SNAP eligibility, some Symbiosis programmes prescribe additional requirements — for example, certain technical MBA programmes may require an engineering background.

Always cross-check both the SNAP eligibility criteria and the specific eligibility requirements of each Symbiosis institute/programme on the current official notification at snaptest.org before registering.

Syllabus

What the SNAP Sections Cover

The topics below represent the major preparation areas commonly tested across SNAP's three sections. Not every topic appears in every session, and the exact mix can vary session to session.

General English

Reading comprehension, vocabulary, grammar, sentence correction and completion, synonyms and antonyms, idioms and phrases, para-jumbles and para-summary, and contextual and inferential understanding.

Analytical & Logical Reasoning

Logical and analytical reasoning, arrangements and seating puzzles, blood relations, direction sense, coding-decoding, number and letter series, syllogisms, statement and conclusion, and critical reasoning.

  • Quantitative, DI & Data Sufficiency — number system, arithmetic (percentages, profit and loss, ratios, averages), time-work, time-speed-distance, interest, algebra, geometry, mensuration, permutation and combination, probability, data interpretation and data sufficiency.
  • All MCQs — all 60 questions are multiple-choice with four options each; there are no descriptive questions.
  • Marking scheme — +1 for every correct answer, -0.25 for every incorrect answer, no penalty for unattempted questions.
  • No sectional timer — candidates may move freely between sections within the overall 60-minute duration.

Common Questions

SNAP Coaching in Dombivli — FAQ

What is the SNAP exam?

SNAP (Symbiosis National Aptitude Test) is a national-level management entrance examination conducted by Symbiosis International (Deemed University) for admission to its MBA and other management programmes.

What is the SNAP exam pattern?

SNAP consists of 60 multiple-choice questions across three sections, to be completed in 60 minutes, for a total of 60 marks, with +1 for a correct answer and -0.25 for an incorrect answer.

What is the SNAP eligibility criteria?

A bachelor's degree in any discipline with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks (45% for SC/ST/PwD candidates). Final-year students may also apply, subject to proof of degree completion.

Can SNAP be taken more than once?

SNAP is typically conducted across multiple sessions/dates within the same admission cycle, with the best score generally used for percentile calculation. Verify current rules on snaptest.org.

Which colleges accept SNAP scores?

SNAP scores are used by participating Symbiosis institutes across several cities, including SIBM Pune, SCMHRD Pune, SIBM Bengaluru and others. Always confirm current participation on snaptest.org.

Does GICE Academy in Dombivli help with SNAP mock tests and sectional practice?

Yes — sectional tests, topic tests and full-length mock tests simulating the real 60-minute interface, along with detailed performance analysis, are built into the preparation journey alongside subject-wise classroom teaching.
