About the Programme

MPSC Preparation Built for Dombivli Graduates

MPSC — the Maharashtra Public Service Commission — is a constitutional body, established under Article 315 of the Constitution of India, responsible for recruiting candidates into Maharashtra's state government services spanning administrative, police, revenue, taxation, clerical and technical roles. Every year, thousands of graduates across the state — including a large number from Dombivli — prepare for MPSC exams in the hope of securing a stable, respected government career.

Because MPSC does not conduct one uniform exam, our Dombivli centre helps candidates first identify their target examination — State Services (Rajyaseva), Group B or Group C — since the eligibility, syllabus and exam pattern differ meaningfully between them. From there, a personalised study plan is built around the actual demands of that exam.

Preparation combines structured subject-wise teaching, a continuous Maharashtra-focused current-affairs habit, previous-year question practice and regular mock tests, together with the mentorship and doubt-solving support that a syllabus of this scale and competitiveness genuinely needs.

Which Exam Are You Targeting

The MPSC Examination Categories

MPSC runs several distinct recruitment processes, each with its own purpose, eligibility, syllabus and selection stages. Candidates should confirm exact, current requirements from the latest official MPSC notification on mpsc.gov.in.

Flagship Exam
State Services (Rajyaseva)

Recruits Class I & II administrative officers — Deputy Collector, DySP, Tahsildar, BDO and similar roles. Objective Prelims followed by a largely descriptive Mains.

Non-Gazetted
Group B (Combined)

Recruits Police Sub-Inspector, State Tax Inspector and Assistant Section Officer. Common Prelims, then one common Main paper and one post-specific Main paper.

Clerical & Technical
Group C (Combined)

Recruits Clerk-Typist, Tax Assistant, Technical Assistant, Industry Inspector and AMVI. Common screening Prelims, cadre-specific Mains, and a typing test where applicable.

Periodic
Other / Departmental Exams

Recruitments for posts like Law Officer, Forest Service, Engineering Services, Medical Officer and Dental Surgeon, announced separately as vacancies arise.

Exact vacancies, posts, eligibility and selection stages for any MPSC examination can vary between notifications. Always verify current details from the official notification on mpsc.gov.in or mpsconline.gov.in.

Who Can Apply

MPSC Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility for MPSC exams depends on the specific examination and post — always confirm exact figures against the relevant notification.

Educational Qualification

Most MPSC examinations require a graduation degree from a recognised university, or an equivalent qualification recognised by the Government of Maharashtra. Industry Inspector and AMVI (Group C) require a specific engineering or science qualification, and some clerical posts require proof of typing proficiency.

Age Limit

Calculated as on a reference date fixed in each notification. As a general pattern, minimum ages are usually around 18–19 years, while maximum ages for open-category candidates commonly range from roughly 31 to 38 years depending on the post — PSI typically has a narrower band. Reserved-category candidates, PwD and ex-servicemen generally receive age relaxation per Maharashtra government rules.

Nationality, Domicile & Language

Generally requires Indian citizenship. Maharashtra domicile is commonly required for state-specific reservation benefits, and working knowledge of Marathi is typically expected for most non-gazetted and clerical posts.

Number of Attempts

MPSC exams do not uniformly restrict the number of attempts — eligibility is generally governed primarily by the age limit rather than a fixed attempt cap, though this can differ by exam and category.

Eligibility conditions differ by examination and post and are published afresh with every notification. Always verify current details from the official MPSC notification before applying.

Syllabus

What the MPSC Exams Cover

The MPSC syllabus differs by examination, but the broad subject areas below are commonly tested across most MPSC exams — always check the post-specific syllabus PDF for your target exam.

Preliminary Syllabus

Current affairs (state, national, international), History (Maharashtra-specific and the Indian National Movement), Geography, Indian Polity, Economy, General Science, Environment, Maharashtra-specific administration and culture, and General Mental Ability / Aptitude.

Mains Syllabus (State Services)

Marathi and English (typically qualifying), Essay/Writing, General Studies covering History and Geography with special reference to Maharashtra, Polity, Economy and agriculture, Science and Technology, Environment and disaster management, and integrated current affairs.

  • Group B Syllabus — Prelims broadly overlaps with the general syllabus; Mains has one common paper (Marathi, English, General Knowledge) plus a post-specific paper for PSI, STI or ASO.
  • Group C Syllabus — Prelims covers General Ability; Mains varies by cadre, with AMVI having its own independent, technically focused paper.
  • Objective format — Prelims and most Mains papers (outside State Services) are objective-type, subject to negative marking.
  • Maharashtra weightage — state history, geography, schemes and current affairs consistently carry meaningful weightage across nearly every MPSC exam.

Common Questions

MPSC Coaching in Dombivli — FAQ

What is MPSC?

MPSC stands for the Maharashtra Public Service Commission, a constitutional body that conducts recruitment examinations and interviews for administrative, police, revenue and technical posts under the Government of Maharashtra.

What is the MPSC syllabus?

It differs by examination. Broadly, it covers Current Affairs, History, Geography, Polity, Economy, General Science and, for Mains-level exams, Marathi, English and Essay writing — the exact syllabus depends on whether you're appearing for State Services, Group B or Group C.

What is the MPSC exam pattern?

There is no single pattern for all MPSC exams. State Services has a two-paper objective Prelims followed by multiple descriptive Mains papers and an Interview. Group B and Group C generally have a single-paper Prelims followed by post-specific Mains papers.

What is the age limit for MPSC?

Age limits vary by exam, post and category, typically calculated as on a reference date set in each notification. Minimum ages are generally around 18–19 years, and maximum ages for open-category candidates commonly fall between roughly 31–38 years. Always confirm the exact limit from the current notification.

How many stages are there in the MPSC exam?

Depending on the exam, stages can include the Preliminary Examination, Main Examination, a Skill/Physical Test where applicable, an Interview where applicable, Document Verification, and a Medical Examination where applicable — not every exam includes every stage.

Does GICE Academy in Dombivli help with MPSC mock tests and previous-year papers?

Yes — structured mock tests, previous-year question-paper practice and guided review are built into the preparation journey alongside subject-wise classroom teaching.
